Mathculus — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

In short: Mathculus runs entirely on your phone. The information you enter is never sent to any server, never shared with any third party, and the app contains no ads or tracking tools.

1. Introduction

Mathculus ("we", "us", "the app") is a mobile application built to help HSC students in Bangladesh practice higher mathematics. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected while using the app, how it is used and stored, and what rights you have regarding it. By using Mathculus, you agree to the terms described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

During the first-time setup (onboarding/profile setup step), the app asks you for the following, so it can be shown on your profile screen:

  • Name
  • College name / Address

In addition, the app automatically generates and stores some usage-related information as you practice, such as:

  • Practice and exam history, and the number of correct/incorrect answers
  • Level, experience points (XP), and progress statistics
  • Which chapter/topic you most recently practiced

Beyond this, Mathculus does not collect your email address, phone number, password, photos, location (GPS), contacts, or any device identifier. The app has no login or sign-up system.

3. Where This Information Is Stored

All of the information above is stored only on your own phone, in the app's local on-device storage. Mathculus has no backend server or database — the app is built to work entirely offline. As a result:

  • The name, address, or practice results you enter are never transmitted over the internet.
  • We (the developers) have no way to see or access this information.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing the app's data/cache from your phone's settings, permanently deletes all of it.

4. Advertising, Analytics, and Third Parties

Mathculus does not use any advertising (ads), analytics, crash-reporting tools, or third-party tracking SDKs (such as Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics/Crashlytics, or any other tracker). The app's fonts and other resources are bundled with the app itself — nothing needs to be fetched from an external server to load them.

5. Children's Privacy

Mathculus is built primarily for HSC students, a significant portion of whom may be under 18. Because information such as name and college/address never leaves the device and never reaches us, we do not collect, store, or process personal information from any user, whether a minor or an adult. Parents or guardians may view or change this information directly from the app's profile screen on their child's phone at any time, or remove it entirely by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.

6. Data Security

Your information is kept in app-specific (sandboxed) storage protected by the operating system, accessible only to the Mathculus app itself. Since information is never transmitted over the internet, there is no risk of it being exposed over a network.

7. Deleting Your Information

Since there is no central account or server-stored data, you can delete all information directly through either of the following:

  • Go to your phone's Settings → Apps → Mathculus → Storage → "Clear data", or
  • Uninstall the app.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if new features are added to the app (for example, if a future version ever needs to collect new information). Any significant change will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Users are encouraged to review this page occasionally.
