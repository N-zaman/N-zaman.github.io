Privacy Policy for My Toolbox BD

Last updated: September 27, 2026

My Toolbox BD ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") is an all-in-one Android tools application that provides calculators, converters, measurement tools, device utilities, document tools, media tools, and other everyday utilities.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the App may access, how that information is used, and how it is handled.

Privacy at a glance: Most tools in My Toolbox BD process information locally on your Android device. We do not require an account, and the App does not sell your personal information.

1. Information We Access

Depending on which tool you use, the App may request access to certain device features or information. Permissions are requested only when a feature requires them.

Camera

Camera access may be used by tools such as QR & Barcode Scanner, Document Scanner, OCR, Magnifier, Color Picker, Heart Rate Monitor, Mirror, and other camera-based features.

Camera input is processed for the requested feature and is not uploaded to our servers by the App.

Microphone

Microphone access may be used by Sound Meter, Voice Recorder, Screen Recorder, and other audio-related features.

Audio is used to provide the requested feature. Voice recordings created by the user are stored locally on the device unless the user explicitly chooses to share or export them using another app.

Location

Location permission may be requested by tools that require location information, including Speedometer, Altitude Meter, Wi-Fi Signal Meter, Network Information, Wi-Fi QR-related features, and other location-dependent utilities.

Location information is used locally to provide the requested functionality. My Toolbox BD does not use your location to build a personal location profile or sell your location information.

Photos and Media

Access to photos or media may be requested by features such as Duplicate Photo Finder, Photo Cleanup, Status Saver, OCR, PDF-related tools, and other media utilities.

These tools use your selected or accessible files to perform the requested operation. The App does not upload your personal photos or documents to our servers for these operations.

Files and Storage

Certain features, including Storage Analyzer, Document Scanner, PDF Toolkit, Photo Cleanup, Status Saver, and Duplicate Photo Finder, may require access to files or shared storage.

Storage access is used only to perform the requested local operation, such as reading, analyzing, creating, modifying, or saving files.

NFC

The NFC Toolkit may use the device's NFC hardware to read or interact with compatible NFC tags and devices.

Device Sensors

Some tools use hardware sensors available on the device, including accelerometer, gyroscope, magnetometer, light sensor, pressure sensor, humidity sensor, step counter, and other supported sensors.

Sensor readings are used locally to provide the corresponding measurement or utility feature.

2. Information Stored Locally

My Toolbox BD stores certain application preferences and user-created information locally on your device. This may include:

  • Favorite and pinned tools
  • Application settings and theme preferences
  • Onboarding status and application preferences
  • Usage-related local preferences, such as rate-prompt settings
  • Notes created using the Notepad tool
  • Other information required for individual tools to function

This information is stored locally using Android application storage and is not transmitted to our servers by the App.

3. Network Information and Public IP Address

The Network Information tool can display network-related information such as connection details and the device's public IP address.

To determine the public IP address, the tool makes a request to the third-party service ipify at https://api.ipify.org .

When this feature is used, the request necessarily reaches the third-party service through your Internet connection. The App does not use the public IP address to create a user profile or store it on our own servers.

For information about ipify, please refer to its website: https://www.ipify.org/

4. Internet Access

My Toolbox BD requires Internet access for limited functionality, such as retrieving the public IP address through the Network Information tool and opening external links, including the application's Google Play Store page.

Most calculators, converters, measurement tools, notes, and other utilities are designed to work locally without sending their input to our servers.

5. QR Codes, Links and Sharing

The QR Scanner can read information encoded in QR codes and barcodes. If a scanned QR code contains a URL and you choose to open it, the URL may be passed to your device's browser or another appropriate application.

Similarly, when you explicitly use Android's share functionality to share a file, image, measurement card, PDF, recording, or other content, that content is handled by the application you select for sharing. The privacy practices of that third-party application then apply.

6. Notifications, Alarms and Background Services

Some tools, including Timer, Stopwatch, Focus Timer, Sleep Timer, and related utilities, may use Android notifications, alarms, vibration, or foreground services to provide their functionality.

These features are used to keep timers running, notify you when a timer finishes, or provide an active service while the corresponding feature is being used.

7. Screen Recording

The Screen Recorder feature uses Android's official MediaProjection mechanism. Android displays a system confirmation before screen recording can begin.

Screen recordings are created on your device. The App does not upload recorded screen content to our servers.

8. Advertising and Analytics

The current version of My Toolbox BD does not intentionally include third-party advertising or analytics services such as Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics, or similar advertising/analytics SDKs.

If advertising, analytics, or other third-party data-processing services are added in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

9. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

The App does not operate a user account system and does not maintain a personal user database.

Some features may intentionally interact with third-party services or applications when you choose to use those features. Examples include:

  • Retrieving public IP information through ipify
  • Opening URLs in a web browser
  • Sharing files or content through Android's sharing system
  • Opening the Google Play Store

10. Data Security

We design the App so that most tool processing occurs locally on your Android device. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your Android device, including its screen lock, operating system, and access to files stored on the device.

11. Children's Privacy

My Toolbox BD does not knowingly collect personal information from children through a user account or registration system.

Because the App does not require an account and most processing is performed locally, we do not intentionally collect children's personal information for profiling or advertising purposes.

12. Your Choices

Android allows you to control permissions granted to the App. You can deny or revoke permissions through your device's system settings.

Some features may not function correctly if the permission required by that feature is denied or revoked.

You can also remove locally stored application data by using the Android system's app-data controls or by uninstalling the App, subject to Android's handling of shared files that you have intentionally saved outside the application's private storage.

13. Third-Party Services

My Toolbox BD may interact with third-party applications or services when you explicitly use functionality that requires them. Such third parties operate under their own privacy policies.

The principal external service currently used by the App for a specific feature is:

  • ipify — used by the Network Information tool to determine the public IP address.
    https://www.ipify.org/

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App, applicable requirements, or our privacy practices.

When changes are made, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be updated. We encourage users to review this page periodically.
