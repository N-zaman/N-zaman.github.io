1. Who we are

Wifi Share (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is published by Utility Bangla Studio. This policy explains what information the app handles, where that information goes, and what you can control. You can reach us at nuruzzaman.dev.bd@gmail.com.

2. What the app does

The app runs a WebDAV file-sharing service on your phone. Other devices on a network you have approved (for example a computer or another phone using a WebDAV-capable file manager) can connect to your phone and work with the folders you selected. Files move directly between your phone and the devices you connect. They are not uploaded to a cloud service operated by us.

Depending on your settings, a connected device that you have authorized can read, upload, replace, rename, move, copy and delete files inside the shared folders. A read-only option is available in the app’s settings.

3. Data we collect

We do not collect personal data. The app has no user accounts and contains no advertising, analytics, attribution or crash-reporting software. It does not transmit your shared files, file names, folder paths, passwords, certificates, network names, IP addresses or location to us or to any third party we operate or control.

The app requests the Android “Internet” permission only because Android requires it for an app to accept connections on your local network. The app does not use it to contact servers operated by us.

Because we do not receive your data, we are generally not able to look up, export, correct or delete it for you. Everything described below is stored on your own device and under your control.

6. Connections, discovery and encryption

Who can connect

The app only shares on networks you approve. It asks you before sharing on a new network, and you can allow it once, allow it permanently or block it. Network approval is a useful restriction, but it is not cryptographic proof of identity: a person can copy a network’s name and access-point address.

Login

Password login (Digest authentication) is optional. If you leave it off, anyone on a network you approved who can reach your phone may access the shared folders. You can turn password login on in the app.

Encrypted and unencrypted connections

  • HTTPS encrypts file transfers, but only protects you if the other device verifies your phone’s certificate. You may need to install or approve the certificate on that device. Check the fingerprint shown in the app before trusting it.
  • HTTP, if you use it, does not encrypt file contents or file-link tokens. Other people on the same network may be able to see or alter the traffic. Use HTTP only on networks you trust.
  • Password login does not encrypt HTTP transfers.

Local discovery

So that other devices can find your phone, the app announces the device name, its network addresses and service details on approved networks, using standard local-network discovery protocols (such as WS-Discovery, SSDP, mDNS and LLMNR). These announcements are not encrypted and can be seen by other devices on that network, even when HTTPS is used for the files.

7. Google Play services

When Android’s Location setting is off, the app can use Google Play services, if available on your device, to show Android’s standard “turn on location” prompt. This only checks and changes the device setting; the app does not request your coordinates. Google Play services is provided by Google and is subject to Google’s Privacy Policy. The app contains no other third-party services.

The app currently contains no advertising.

8. Storage, backup and deletion

  • The app’s settings, credentials, network decisions and file-property database are excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. After moving to a new phone, you will need to set the app up again.
  • Saved network rules and folder selections stay until you remove them, clear the app’s data or uninstall the app.
  • Using “Reset credentials” creates a new password and invalidates old access. Resetting the security certificate means other devices must trust the new one.
  • Clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the app does not delete the files you shared; they remain in your phone’s storage.
